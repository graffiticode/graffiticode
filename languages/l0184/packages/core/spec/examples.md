# L0184 RAG Training Examples

92 example prompts for training a RAG model on L0184, the charts language — covering bar, line,
pie, donut, rose and scatter charts, combined bar-and-line charts with a second axis, tables of
data that several charts share, collections of charts shown as tabs, horizontal, logarithmic,
time and bounded axes, titles, legends, labels, colours and themes, and missing values.

Each numbered line is a prompt in the author's own voice. Prompts describe WHAT to chart and the
numbers to chart, never how to write the program. Every prompt here is in scope — fetching data
from a URL, histograms, heatmaps, maps and network charts belong elsewhere or are not built yet,
and are deliberately absent.

## Category 1: Bar Charts (1–12)

1. Bar chart of monthly revenue: January 120, February 132, March 101, April 134.
2. Show the number of students in each grade: 6th grade 210, 7th grade 198, 8th grade 225.
3. Make a bar chart of the five most populous US states in millions: California 39.0, Texas 30.5, Florida 22.6, New York 19.6, Pennsylvania 13.0.
4. Compare 2025 and 2026 sales side by side for North, South, East and West: 2025 was 40, 32, 28, 35 and 2026 was 44, 30, 35, 38.
5. Stacked bars of energy use by source for three years: coal 30, 26, 20; gas 40, 42, 43; renewables 20, 26, 33.
6. A bar chart of daily steps for a week: 8200, 10400, 7600, 12100, 9800, 15300, 6400, Monday to Sunday.
7. Chart the number of books read per month by our class: 14, 18, 22, 17, 25, 30.
8. Bar chart of average rainfall in millimetres for the four seasons: winter 78, spring 62, summer 41, autumn 85.
9. Show votes for each lunch option: pizza 42, tacos 35, pasta 28, salad 12, sushi 19.
10. Make each bar a different colour for the medals: gold 10, silver 14, bronze 9.
11. Grouped bars of test scores for boys and girls in Math, Reading and Science: boys 78, 74, 81 and girls 80, 83, 79.
12. Stacked bars showing how many tickets were open, in progress and closed each week for four weeks: open 12, 9, 14, 7; in progress 8, 11, 6, 10; closed 20, 24, 28, 31.
